## The Work of the Perfect Hostess
“完美女主人”的劳动

## Today Card｜首页卡片版

Clarissa Dalloway spends one June day preparing an evening party. She buys flowers, considers the guest list, repairs her dress, receives visitors, and worries whether the gathering will succeed. These activities are easily dismissed as the trivial preoccupations of a politician's wife. Virginia Woolf instead enters Clarissa's consciousness and asks what kind of work is hidden inside the role of the “perfect hostess.”

克拉丽莎·达洛维用六月里的一天准备晚间宴会。她购买鲜花、考虑宾客名单、缝补礼服、接待来客，也担心聚会能否成功。这些活动很容易被视为一位政治家妻子的琐碎事务。弗吉尼亚·伍尔夫却进入克拉丽莎的意识，追问“完美女主人”这个角色隐藏了怎样的劳动。

Clarissa's party is both personal and political. It is her attempt to bring separated lives into one room, but it also sustains the social world of her husband, Richard, and the governing class to which they belong. Her work creates connection, atmosphere, and recognition without receiving the name of a profession.

克拉丽莎的宴会既是私人活动，也是政治活动。她试图把彼此分离的生命带进同一个房间，而聚会同时维系了丈夫理查德及其所属统治阶层的社交世界。她制造联系、气氛与相互承认，这些工作却没有获得职业的名称。

The novel also prevents us from treating Clarissa as the only woman working in the house. Her freedom to walk through London and imagine the party as her creative gift depends on servants whose cooking, cleaning, carrying, dressing, and door-keeping make the evening possible. A feminist reading of the housewife must therefore keep gender and class in the same frame.

小说也阻止我们把克拉丽莎当作这所房子里唯一劳动的女人。她能够走上伦敦街头，并把宴会想象成自己的创造性赠礼，是因为佣人承担了烹饪、清洁、搬运、协助穿衣与看门等工作。对家庭主妇的女性主义阅读，因此必须把性别与阶级放在同一个画面里。

## A Novel Built Around a Woman's Day
一部围绕女性日常建立的小说

Mrs Dalloway was published by the Hogarth Press in 1925. Most of its present-day action unfolds across one day in postwar London: Clarissa Dalloway prepares an evening party while the novel moves among streets, shops, drawing rooms, government offices, medical consulting rooms, memories, and other people's minds. A parallel narrative follows Septimus Warren Smith, a veteran whose suffering is interpreted and controlled by doctors until he dies by suicide.

《达洛维夫人》于1925年由霍加斯出版社出版。小说当下的大部分行动发生在战后伦敦的一天之内：克拉丽莎·达洛维准备晚间宴会，叙事则穿行于街道、商店、客厅、政府办公室、诊室、记忆与不同人物的意识之间。另一条平行叙事追随退伍军人塞普蒂默斯·沃伦·史密斯；他的痛苦不断受到医生的解释与控制，直至他以自杀结束生命。

The action assigned to Clarissa is deliberately ordinary. She is not beginning a profession, leading a political campaign, or publishing a book. She is giving a party. By placing this feminized social activity at the centre of a modernist novel, Woolf asks readers to look again at forms of work that disappear when public achievement is treated as the only serious action.

小说分配给克拉丽莎的行动有意保持普通。她没有开始一项职业，没有领导政治运动，也没有出版一本书；她只是在举办宴会。伍尔夫把这种女性化的社交活动放在一部现代主义小说的中心，要求读者重新观看那些容易消失的劳动形式——因为传统标准往往只把公共成就视为严肃行动。

## “Mrs Richard Dalloway”｜“理查德·达洛维夫人”

The title names Clarissa through marriage before the first sentence begins. As she walks through Bond Street, she experiences herself as no longer simply Clarissa but as “Mrs Richard Dalloway.” The married name records social position, respectability, and access. It also reveals how a wife's public identity can become an extension of her husband's.

小说标题在第一句话开始之前，已经通过婚姻为克拉丽莎命名。当她走过邦德街时，她感觉自己不再只是克拉丽莎，而是“理查德·达洛维夫人”。婚后姓名记录着社会位置、体面与准入资格，同时也揭示妻子的公共身份如何成为丈夫身份的延伸。

This does not mean that Clarissa possesses no inner life or agency. Woolf's narrative technique demonstrates precisely the opposite: beneath a conventional social name exists a dense field of memory, desire, judgment, fear, bodily sensation, and philosophical thought. The tension lies between the complexity Woolf gives her and the abbreviated identity society recognizes.

这并不意味着克拉丽莎没有内在生活或能动性。伍尔夫的叙事技巧恰恰证明了相反的一点：在一个传统的社会称谓之下，存在着由记忆、欲望、判断、恐惧、身体感受与哲学思考构成的密集世界。真正的张力发生在伍尔夫赋予她的复杂性与社会承认的简化身份之间。

Her marriage to Richard offers privacy, material security, and a measure of emotional distance that she values. Yet it also places her inside a political and class formation whose assumptions she sometimes questions but whose advantages she continues to inhabit. Woolf does not reduce marriage to either imprisonment or fulfilment; she shows it as a structure that protects, limits, and names Clarissa at the same time.

她与理查德的婚姻提供了隐私、物质安全，以及她所珍视的一定情感距离。但婚姻也把她放进一套政治与阶级结构之中：她有时质疑这套结构的假设，却仍然生活在它提供的优势里。伍尔夫没有把婚姻简化成监禁或圆满，而是把它写成一个同时保护、限制并命名克拉丽莎的结构。

## Buying the Flowers Herself｜亲自去买花

The novel's opening sentence gives Clarissa a small decision: she will buy the flowers herself. The word “herself” briefly separates her from the household role and sends her into the city as a moving, perceiving subject. Her walk is pleasurable, sensory, and intellectually active. Domestic preparation becomes the occasion for access to public London.

小说开篇让克拉丽莎作出一个很小的决定：她要亲自去买花。“亲自”这个词短暂地把她从家庭角色中分离出来，让她作为一个移动、观看与感受的主体进入城市。她的步行充满愉悦、感官经验与思想活动；家庭准备工作因此成为她进入公共伦敦的契机。

But the next sentence changes the frame: Lucy already has her own work to do. Clarissa's excursion is possible because household labour has been delegated. The contrast is crucial. Clarissa's “work” can appear as choice, taste, and self-expression, while Lucy's work appears as necessity and remains largely outside the narrative centre.

但紧接着的下一句话改变了画面：露西已经有自己的工作要做。克拉丽莎之所以能够外出，是因为家务劳动已被委托给别人。这个对比十分关键：克拉丽莎的“工作”可以表现为选择、品味与自我表达，露西的劳动却表现为必需事务，并且大多留在叙事中心之外。

Calling Clarissa a housewife without qualification would therefore be misleading. She is an upper-class wife and society hostess who manages a household supported by paid domestic workers. The novel's domestic world contains a hierarchy among women, not a single shared female condition.

因此，不加限定地把克拉丽莎称作家庭主妇会造成误导。她是一位上层阶级妻子与社交女主人，管理着一套由有薪家务劳动者支撑的家庭。小说中的家庭世界包含女性之间的等级，而不是一种所有女性共同拥有的单一处境。

## The Perfect Hostess｜完美女主人

Peter Walsh once predicts that Clarissa will marry a prime minister and become the “perfect hostess.” Clarissa cries after hearing it, and years later she still recoils when he repeats the judgment. The phrase diminishes her by suggesting that her future contains no independent intellectual or artistic vocation. Her achievement will be to stand at the top of a staircase and make other people comfortable.

彼得·沃尔什曾预言，克拉丽莎会嫁给首相，并成为“完美女主人”。克拉丽莎听后哭了；多年以后，当彼得重复这个判断时，她仍然全身退缩。这个称呼缩小了她，因为它暗示她的未来不会拥有独立的思想或艺术志业；她的成就只是站在楼梯顶端，让别人感到自在。

Yet Peter's phrase also names a real capacity. He observes Clarissa's courage, social instinct, manners, and ability to carry an event through. Hostessing requires attention: remembering relationships, reading discomfort, introducing strangers, controlling pace, moving between groups, and sustaining a collective mood. These skills are treated as natural femininity precisely when they are performed successfully.

然而，彼得的称呼也确实命名了一种真实能力。他注意到克拉丽莎的勇气、社交直觉、礼仪，以及把一场活动贯彻到底的能力。招待宾客需要持续注意：记住关系、辨认不适、介绍陌生人、控制节奏、穿行于不同群体，并维持共同气氛。正因为这些能力被成功执行，它们才容易被当成女性天性，而不是劳动。

The label is therefore double. It confines Clarissa to a gendered social function, but it cannot fully erase the intelligence exercised through that function. Woolf holds together the insult and the skill rather than asking us to choose only one.

因此，这个称呼具有双重性。它把克拉丽莎限制在一种性别化的社会功能里，却无法彻底抹去她通过这一功能运用的智慧。伍尔夫让侮辱与能力同时存在，而不是要求读者只选择其中一种解释。

## The Party as Unnamed Social Labour｜作为无名社会劳动的宴会

Clarissa cannot give Peter a practical explanation for her parties. She calls them an offering. She imagines people living separately across London and feels the waste of their isolation; the party is her attempt to bring them together. In her own account, hosting is a creative practice of relation.

克拉丽莎无法向彼得提供一个关于宴会的实用解释。她把宴会称作一种赠礼。她想象人们分散生活在伦敦各处，并为彼此隔绝感到惋惜；宴会是她试图让他们相遇的方式。在她自己的理解中，招待宾客是一种创造关系的实践。

Jacob Littleton's influential essay reads Clarissa as a middle-aged woman artist whose gatherings transform private feeling into a public act. This interpretation does not require us to pretend that a party is politically innocent. It asks why connection, arrangement, timing, and atmosphere cease to count as artistry when they are produced inside a home by a wife.

Jacob Littleton 一篇具有影响力的论文把克拉丽莎读作一位中年女性艺术家；她通过聚会把私人感受转化成公共行动。这种解释并不要求我们假装宴会在政治上无辜。它追问的是：当联系、安排、时机与气氛由一位妻子在家庭内部创造时，为什么这些能力不再被算作艺术？

The party can also be understood as affective and social labour. Clarissa produces no object that can be possessed after the guests leave. She produces an encounter: a temporary arrangement in which people see one another, recognize status, renew acquaintances, exchange information, and experience themselves as part of a social world.

宴会也可以被理解为情感劳动与社会劳动。客人离开之后，克拉丽莎并没有生产出一个可以被占有的物件；她生产的是一次相遇：人们在一个临时安排中彼此观看、确认地位、重续关系、交换信息，并感受到自己属于某个社会世界。

## A Private Party with Public Consequences｜一场具有公共后果的私人宴会

The Dalloways' home does not stand outside politics. Richard is a member of Parliament, the Prime Minister attends the party, and guests discuss government, empire, legislation, medicine, and war. Clarissa's domestic hospitality creates an informal space in which the political class recognizes and reproduces itself.

达洛维家的住宅并不位于政治之外。理查德是议会议员，首相出席宴会，宾客谈论政府、帝国、立法、医学与战争。克拉丽莎的家庭招待创造了一个非正式空间，让政治阶层在其中确认并再生产自身。

Alex Zwerdling's study of the novel's “social system” is important here. It directs attention away from treating the book as pure private consciousness and toward its criticism of class authority, imperial assumptions, governing institutions, and professional power. Clarissa's party belongs to that system even when she experiences it as a personal gift.

Alex Zwerdling 对小说“社会系统”的研究在这里尤其重要。它让我们不再把这本书只当作纯粹的私人意识书写，而是注意其中对阶级权威、帝国假设、治理机构与专业权力的批评。即使克拉丽莎把宴会体验为个人赠礼，它仍然属于这套系统。

This tension prevents a simple celebration of hostess work. The ability to gather people may create genuine connection, while the particular gathering also consolidates an unequal social order. Care and power do not occupy separate rooms.

这种张力阻止我们简单赞美女主人的劳动。聚集他人的能力可以创造真实联系，而这场具体聚会也在巩固不平等的社会秩序。照护与权力并不位于两个彼此分离的房间。

## The Women Who Make the Party Possible｜使宴会成为可能的女性

The evening depends on a larger workforce than Clarissa. Lucy prepares the house. Mrs Walker manages the cooking under intense pressure. Mrs Parkinson is hired for parties, and Ellen Barnet returns to help women with their clothes. Rumpelmayer's workers alter the doors, while additional hands move food, coffee, coats, and guests through the house.

晚间宴会依赖一支比克拉丽莎更庞大的劳动队伍。露西准备住宅，沃克太太在巨大压力下管理烹饪，帕金森太太受雇为宴会服务，艾伦·巴内特则回来协助女宾整理衣物。Rumpelmayer 的工人拆卸房门，其他劳动者让食物、咖啡、外套与宾客在住宅中顺利流动。

Mary Wilson's scholarship on Woolf, Katherine Mansfield, and their servants argues that domestic workers were material contributors to modernist production because their housekeeping made other women's artistic work possible. The same question can be brought inside Mrs Dalloway. If Clarissa's party is an artwork, whose labour supplies its unseen infrastructure?

Mary Wilson 关于伍尔夫、凯瑟琳·曼斯菲尔德及其佣人的研究指出，家务劳动者是现代主义生产的物质贡献者，因为她们的劳动使其他女性的艺术工作成为可能。同一个问题也可以被带进《达洛维夫人》内部：如果克拉丽莎的宴会是一件艺术作品，那么是谁的劳动构成了它不可见的基础设施？

The servants are not given the same narrative depth as Clarissa. That inequality should not be repaired by inventing thoughts for them. It should be marked as part of the novel's distribution of visibility: upper-class feminine interiority becomes the centre, while working women's bodies and tasks remain necessary but partial presences.

佣人没有获得与克拉丽莎同等的叙事深度。我们不应通过替她们虚构思想来修补这种不平等，而应把它标记为小说分配可见性的方式：上层女性的内在生活成为中心，劳动女性的身体与任务不可缺少，却只以局部形态出现。

## Domestic Femininity Is Performed, Not Natural｜家庭女性气质是一种表演，而非天性

Francisco José Cortés Vieco's comparison of Mrs Dalloway with Katherine Mansfield's “The Garden Party” examines how bourgeois hostesses inherit Victorian domestic femininity, social etiquette, and class consciousness. This framework helps explain why Clarissa both chooses and suffers the role. The performance has been learned so thoroughly that skill can look like nature.

Francisco José Cortés Vieco 把《达洛维夫人》与凯瑟琳·曼斯菲尔德的《花园茶会》并置，讨论资产阶级女主人如何继承维多利亚时代的家庭女性气质、社交礼仪与阶级意识。这个框架有助于解释克拉丽莎为什么既选择这一角色，又承受它的限制：表演被学习得如此熟练，以至于能力看起来像天性。

The party gives Clarissa a public function without granting her a public profession. She can exercise judgment and authority as long as these remain attached to hospitality, marriage, and the home. Her social labour is permitted because it appears to support other people's importance rather than announce her own.

宴会给予克拉丽莎一种公共功能，却没有给予她一项公共职业。只要判断与权威仍然依附于待客、婚姻与家庭，她就可以运用它们。她的社会劳动之所以得到允许，是因为它看起来在支持别人的重要性，而不是宣布她自己的重要性。

This is one way the housewife role organizes ability. It does not always suppress women's capacities outright. It may use those capacities intensively while refusing to recognize them as independent work.

这正是家庭主妇角色组织能力的一种方式。它并不总是直接压抑女性的才能，也可能高强度地使用这些才能，同时拒绝把它们承认为独立工作。

## The Home Opens onto the City｜家庭向城市敞开

Clarissa moves between private rooms and public streets rather than remaining sealed inside the home. Susan Merrill Squier's study of Woolf's London argues that Woolf's mature writing reworks a patriarchal city through female movement and perception. Clarissa's walk allows a married woman to read the city, remember earlier selves, and encounter public spectacle.

克拉丽莎在私人房间与公共街道之间移动，而不是被封闭在家庭内部。Susan Merrill Squier 对伍尔夫笔下伦敦的研究指出，伍尔夫成熟时期的写作通过女性的移动与感知，重新处理一座父权城市。克拉丽莎的步行让一位已婚女性阅读城市、回忆早先的自我，并接触公共景观。

Anna Snaith's work on public and private negotiation likewise cautions against treating these categories as fixed opposites in Woolf. Clarissa takes domestic purpose into the street and brings public London back into her drawing room. The house is not outside history; the city is not free of domestic identities.

Anna Snaith 关于公共与私人协商的研究同样提醒我们，不应把这两个范畴视为伍尔夫作品中固定的对立面。克拉丽莎带着家庭目的走上街头，又把公共伦敦带回客厅。家庭并不位于历史之外，城市也没有摆脱家庭身份。

Her mobility remains classed. Clarissa shops in Bond Street, lives in Westminster, and can convert urban contacts into a prestigious gathering. The freedom represented by her walk is real, but it is not universally available to every woman in the novel.

她的移动能力仍然带有阶级性质。克拉丽莎在邦德街购物，居住在威斯敏斯特，并能把城市中的人际接触转化成一场享有声望的聚会。她的步行所代表的自由是真实的，却并非小说中每一位女性都能拥有的普遍自由。

## When Death Enters the Party｜当死亡进入宴会

Clarissa and Septimus never meet. News of his death arrives through Sir William Bradshaw, the celebrated doctor whose authority has helped make Septimus's life unlivable. The party therefore receives not only a report of private tragedy but also the consequence of a public institution entering a domestic gathering.

克拉丽莎与塞普蒂默斯从未见面。他死亡的消息由名医威廉·布拉德肖爵士带进宴会；正是这位医生所代表的权威，使塞普蒂默斯的生活变得无法继续。因此，宴会接收到的不只是私人悲剧的报道，也是公共制度后果进入家庭聚会的时刻。

Clarissa briefly withdraws from her guests. She refuses to absorb the death smoothly into polite conversation and imagines the stranger's act as a form of resistance against the people who forced themselves into his soul. For a moment, the hostess stops maintaining atmosphere. Death interrupts the social surface she has worked to create.

克拉丽莎短暂离开宾客。她拒绝让这场死亡顺滑地变成礼貌谈资，并把陌生人的行动想象成对那些强行进入他灵魂之人的抵抗。在这一刻，女主人停止维持气氛；死亡打断了她辛苦创造的社交表面。

She nevertheless returns to the party. This return can be read neither as simple moral failure nor as complete transcendence. The gathering continues, but it has been altered by a life excluded from it. Woolf places the beauty of connection beside the violence that respectable society prefers not to see.

但她最终仍然回到宴会。这个返回既不能被简单理解为道德失败，也不是彻底超越。聚会继续进行，却已经被一个遭其排除的生命改变。伍尔夫把连接之美与体面社会不愿观看的暴力并置在一起。

## Is Hosting a Vocation?｜招待宾客是一种志业吗？

The novel gives no stable answer. Clarissa says that her parties are the only gift she possesses, while the narration also records vanity, exclusion, political prestige, and her dependence on servants. Her hospitality is creative without being innocent, skilled without being professionally recognized, and intimate without being merely private.

小说没有给出稳定答案。克拉丽莎说，宴会是她唯一拥有的天赋；叙事同时记录虚荣、排斥、政治声望，以及她对佣人劳动的依赖。她的待客具有创造性，却并不无辜；需要能力，却不被承认为职业；属于亲密生活，却并非纯粹私人事务。

This ambiguity is the value of approaching Mrs Dalloway through the figure of the housewife. The novel does not ask us simply to rescue domestic labour by declaring it beautiful, nor to dismiss it as empty convention. It asks how women's capacities are absorbed into marriage and class, how relation-making becomes labour, and how one woman's creative freedom can rest on another woman's less visible work.

这正是从家庭主妇方向阅读《达洛维夫人》的价值。小说既不要求我们只需宣布家务美好，就能拯救家庭劳动，也不允许我们把它视为空洞传统而一笔勾销。它追问女性能力如何被婚姻与阶级吸收，制造关系如何成为劳动，以及一个女人的创造自由如何建立在另一个女人较少被看见的工作之上。

## Quotation｜引文

> “Mrs. Dalloway said she would buy the flowers herself.”

> “达洛维夫人说，花还是由她亲自去买。”

— Virginia Woolf, Mrs Dalloway

The opening looks like a declaration of independence, but its meaning changes when followed by Lucy's workload. “Herself” gives Clarissa movement and choice; it also marks the domestic hierarchy that makes such choice possible.

这个开篇看起来像一项独立宣言，但当露西的工作紧随其后出现时，它的意义随之改变。“亲自”给予克拉丽莎行动与选择，同时也标记出让这种选择成为可能的家庭等级。

> “They're an offering.”

> “它们是一份赠礼。”

— Virginia Woolf, Mrs Dalloway

This is Clarissa's answer to the imagined question of what her parties are for. It refuses economic usefulness as the only measure of value. Yet the gift remains socially situated: it brings people together inside an unequal world rather than standing beyond that world.

这是克拉丽莎对“宴会究竟有什么用”这一想象中问题的回答。它拒绝把经济用途当作衡量价值的唯一尺度。但这份赠礼仍然处于具体社会位置之中：它让人们在一个不平等世界内部相遇，而不是站在那个世界之外。

## Scholarship｜学术文献

### Alex Zwerdling, “Mrs. Dalloway and the Social System”

PMLA, vol. 92, no. 1, January 1977, pp. 69–82. DOI: [10.2307/461415](https://doi.org/10.2307/461415).

Zwerdling establishes a major social and political reading of the novel. His essay is essential for understanding the Dalloways' household, Clarissa's party, medicine, empire, and government as parts of one social system rather than as unrelated background to private consciousness.

Zwerdling 建立了这部小说重要的社会政治阅读路径。他的论文有助于把达洛维家的住宅、克拉丽莎的宴会、医学、帝国与政府理解为同一个社会系统的组成部分，而不是私人意识之外彼此无关的背景。

### Jacob Littleton, “Mrs. Dalloway: Portrait of the Artist as a Middle-Aged Woman”

Twentieth Century Literature, vol. 41, no. 1, Spring 1995, pp. 36–53. JSTOR: [stable/441714](https://www.jstor.org/stable/441714).

Littleton argues that Clarissa's social gatherings can be read as creative public acts. This provides the strongest scholarly basis for treating hostess work as artistry, while the present calendar essay adds a labour and class question: who supports that artistry inside the household?

Littleton 主张，可以把克拉丽莎的社交聚会读作创造性的公共行动。这为把女主人工作理解成艺术提供了重要学术依据；本期日历则进一步加入劳动与阶级问题：家庭内部是谁在支撑这种艺术？

### Francisco José Cortés Vieco, “(Im)perfect Celebrations by Intergenerational Hostesses”

International Journal of English Studies, vol. 20, no. 1, 2020, pp. 93–111. DOI: [10.6018/ijes.364191](https://doi.org/10.6018/ijes.364191).

This article compares Clarissa Dalloway with Laura Sheridan in Katherine Mansfield's “The Garden Party.” It examines bourgeois women as hostesses shaped by social etiquette, class consciousness, and inherited domestic femininity, while showing how death disturbs the celebration.

这篇论文把克拉丽莎·达洛维与凯瑟琳·曼斯菲尔德《花园茶会》中的劳拉·谢里丹并置，讨论资产阶级女性如何在社交礼仪、阶级意识与继承而来的家庭女性气质中成为女主人，也分析死亡如何扰乱庆典。

### Mary Wilson, “Who Is My Contemporary?: Woolf, Mansfield, and Their Servants”

In Virginia Woolf and Her Female Contemporaries, edited by Julie Vandivere and Megan Hicks, Liverpool University Press, 2016, pp. 8–14. [Cambridge Core record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/virginia-woolf-and-her-female-contemporaries/who-is-my-contemporary-woolf-mansfield-and-their-servants/16E870748DE176881B653E624990D932).

Wilson places domestic servants inside the material history of women's modernist writing. Her chapter supports this entry's decision not to discuss Clarissa's creativity without also naming the paid household labour that creates time, order, and space for upper-class and literary women.

Wilson 把家务佣人带进女性现代主义写作的物质历史。她的研究支持本期文案的一个基本决定：讨论克拉丽莎的创造力时，也必须命名那些为上层女性与女性作家创造时间、秩序与空间的有薪家务劳动。

### Emily Blair, Virginia Woolf and the Nineteenth-Century Domestic Novel

State University of New York Press, 2007. [Publisher description](https://utpdistribution.com/9780791471203/virginia-woolf-and-the-nineteenth-century-domestic-novel/).

Blair studies Woolf's sustained and conflicted engagement with nineteenth-century domesticity, conduct literature, household management, and female creativity. The book provides historical context for reading Clarissa as both a modern woman and an inheritor of older domestic forms.

Blair 研究伍尔夫与十九世纪家庭生活、行为规范文本、家庭管理及女性创造力之间持续而矛盾的关系。这本书为理解克拉丽莎提供历史语境：她既是一位现代女性，也是较早家庭形式的继承者。

### Anna Snaith, Virginia Woolf: Public and Private Negotiations

Palgrave Macmillan, 2000. DOI: [10.1007/978-0-230-28794-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-230-28794-5).

Snaith examines how Woolf's feminism, narrative form, authorship, and public interventions repeatedly negotiate rather than simply oppose public and private life. This is useful for understanding why Clarissa's home is a political space and why her city walk remains connected to domestic purpose.

Snaith 讨论伍尔夫的女性主义、叙事形式、作者身份与公共介入如何不断协商，而不是简单对立公共与私人生活。这有助于理解为什么克拉丽莎的住宅是一处政治空间，也解释她的城市步行为什么始终与家庭目的相连。

### Susan Merrill Squier, Virginia Woolf and London: The Sexual Politics of the City

University of North Carolina Press, originally published 1985; enduring edition 2011. [UNC Press record](https://uncpress.org/9780807865965/virginia-woolf-and-london/).

Squier examines London across Woolf's essays and fiction, including Mrs Dalloway, and argues that Woolf reworks a masculine cultural city through female perception and creative “scene making.” Her study supports the reading of Clarissa's walk as an encounter between married identity and public urban life.

Squier 研究伍尔夫随笔与小说中的伦敦，包括《达洛维夫人》，并指出伍尔夫通过女性感知与创造性的“场景制造”重新处理男性文化城市。她的研究支持把克拉丽莎的步行理解成婚姻身份与公共城市生活的相遇。

## Related Works｜相关作品

- **Mrs Dalloway's Party｜《达洛维夫人的宴会》**

  Virginia Woolf's connected short-story sequence offers additional views of Clarissa's social world and the people gathered around her parties.

  伍尔夫相互关联的短篇小说组，从更多角度呈现克拉丽莎的社交世界，以及聚集在她宴会周围的人。

- **To the Lighthouse｜《到灯塔去》**

  Woolf's novel extends the question of how a woman's emotional, domestic, and relational labour sustains a household while remaining difficult to name as work.

  伍尔夫在这部小说中继续追问：一位女性的情感、家庭与关系劳动如何维系家庭，却难以被命名为工作。

- **A Room of One's Own｜《一间自己的房间》**

  Woolf's essay makes material conditions central to women's intellectual freedom, offering a useful counterpoint to Clarissa's wealth, domestic space, and lack of a recognized vocation.

  伍尔夫在这篇随笔中把物质条件置于女性思想自由的中心，可与克拉丽莎拥有财富与家庭空间、却缺少被承认志业的处境对读。

- **The Garden Party｜《花园茶会》**

  Katherine Mansfield's story also places a bourgeois girl's party preparation beside class inequality and an unexpected death.

  凯瑟琳·曼斯菲尔德的短篇同样把资产阶级女孩的宴会准备与阶级不平等及意外死亡并置。
