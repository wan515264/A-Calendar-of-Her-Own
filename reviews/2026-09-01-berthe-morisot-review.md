# 9月1日｜Berthe Morisot

## Berthe Morisot

## 贝尔特·莫里索

**Artist / Impressionism / Women and Modernity｜艺术家 / 印象派 / 女性与现代性**

## A Woman at the Centre of Impressionism

## 站在印象派中心的女性

## Today Card｜首页卡片版

Berthe Morisot should not be remembered merely as a woman who knew the Impressionists, modelled for Édouard Manet, or married his brother. She was a founding participant in the independent exhibition that later became known as the first Impressionist exhibition. In 1874 she was the only woman among its thirty artists. She went on to exhibit in seven of the group's eight exhibitions and contributed financially to sustaining the movement.

贝尔特·莫里索不应只被记作一位认识印象派画家、曾为 Édouard Manet 担任模特，或嫁给他弟弟的女人。她是后来被称为“第一届印象派展览”的那场独立展览的创始参与者。1874年，她是三十位参展艺术家中唯一的女性。此后，她参加了印象派八届展览中的七届，并曾出资维持这一运动。

Morisot painted the modern world from spaces structured by gender and class: drawing rooms, bedrooms, balconies, gardens, parks, coastal resorts, and boats. These subjects were sometimes dismissed as private or feminine. Yet her rapid brushwork, cropped compositions, pale grounds, unfinished edges, and unstable boundaries turned those spaces into laboratories of Impressionist painting.

莫里索从受到性别与阶级塑造的空间观看现代世界：客厅、卧室、阳台、花园、公园、海滨度假地与船只。这些题材曾被贬低为私人或女性化的领域，但她迅疾的笔触、截切式构图、浅色底层、未封闭的边缘与不稳定的界线，却把这些空间变成了印象派绘画的实验室。

Her importance does not lie in proving that a woman could paint exactly like the men around her. It lies in showing that modernity looked different according to who was allowed to move through it, who could look without danger, and whose daily life art history had considered worthy of attention.

她的重要性不在于证明一个女人能够画得与周围男性完全相同，而在于表明：一个人被允许去往何处、能否安全观看，以及谁的日常生活被艺术史视为值得描绘，都会改变现代性的面貌。

> “I will gain my independence only through perseverance and by openly declaring my intention to emancipate myself.”
>
> “我只有依靠坚持，并公开表明我要获得解放的意志，才能取得自己的独立。”

— Berthe Morisot, 1871; translated from the French

## Detail Page｜详细介绍页

### Berthe Morisot｜贝尔特·莫里索

### 1841–1895

### Painter / Printmaker｜画家 / 版画家

Berthe Marie Pauline Morisot was born in Bourges, France, in 1841, into an affluent bourgeois family receptive to art and culture. She and her sister Edma received private art instruction, copied works in the Louvre, and studied landscape painting with artists connected to the Barbizon tradition, including Camille Corot.

Berthe Marie Pauline Morisot 于1841年出生在法国布尔日一个重视艺术与文化的富裕资产阶级家庭。她与姐姐 Edma 接受私人艺术教育，在卢浮宫临摹作品，并跟随包括 Camille Corot 在内、与巴比松传统有关的艺术家学习风景画。

This private route was not simply a family preference. The state-sponsored École des Beaux-Arts did not admit women until 1897, two years after Morisot's death. Women seeking serious training had to depend on private teachers and academies, often at considerable expense, and remained excluded from important competitions and institutional pathways available to men.

这条私人教育路径并不只是家庭偏好。由国家支持的巴黎美术学院直到1897年才开始招收女性，那已经是莫里索去世两年以后。希望接受严肃训练的女性只能依赖私人教师与收费高昂的私立学院，也无法进入许多向男性开放的重要竞赛与制度路径。

Morisot's class privilege gave her resources that most women did not possess, including time, instruction, family connections, and access to the Louvre. It did not remove the gendered boundary between a respectable feminine accomplishment and a public professional identity. Her decision to call herself a painter and exhibit her work therefore remained socially consequential.

莫里索的阶级特权为她提供了大多数女性无法拥有的资源，包括时间、教育、家庭关系与进入卢浮宫学习的机会。但这些条件并没有消除“体面女性的业余才艺”与公共职业身份之间的性别界线。因此，她决定把自己确立为画家并公开展出作品，仍然具有重要的社会意义。

## From the Salon to an Independent Exhibition｜从官方沙龙走向独立展览

Morisot first exhibited at the official Paris Salon in 1864 and continued to submit work during the following decade. The Salon offered visibility, but its jury and academic hierarchy also helped determine which subjects, techniques, and careers could receive official legitimacy.

莫里索于1864年首次参加巴黎官方沙龙，并在随后十年间持续送展。沙龙能够带来可见度，但它的评审制度与学院等级也在决定哪些题材、技法与艺术生涯可以获得官方合法性。

In 1874 she chose the riskier route. Against the advice of Édouard Manet and Pierre Puvis de Chavannes, she joined the Société Anonyme des Artistes Peintres, Sculpteurs, Graveurs and exhibited in the independent show held in the former studio of the photographer Nadar. The group did not yet present one unified style, and the term “Impressionist” was initially attached to it through hostile criticism.

1874年，她选择了一条风险更大的道路。尽管 Édouard Manet 与 Pierre Puvis de Chavannes 并不赞成，她仍加入了“画家、雕塑家与版画家匿名协会”，并参加在摄影师 Nadar 原工作室举办的独立展览。这个团体当时尚未形成完全统一的风格，“印象派”一词最初也是通过带有敌意的批评被加到他们身上的。

Morisot was not a marginal guest. The National Gallery of Art records that she showed in seven of the eight Impressionist exhibitions and contributed money to support the movement. Her one absence, in 1879, followed the birth of her daughter Julie the previous year.

莫里索并不是边缘性的客人。美国国家美术馆的资料显示，她参加了印象派八届展览中的七届，并出资支持这一运动。她唯一缺席的是1879年展览，此前一年，她生下女儿 Julie。

To describe her simply as “the female Impressionist” can isolate her from the movement she helped make. A more accurate formulation is that she was an Impressionist whose career also reveals how differently avant-garde participation was lived by a woman.

仅仅把她称为“那位女性印象派画家”，可能反而把她从自己参与塑造的运动中隔离出来。更准确的说法是：她是一位印象派画家，而她的生涯也揭示了女性参与先锋艺术时必须面对的不同条件。

## The Cradle: Intimacy Is Not a Minor Subject｜《摇篮》：亲密不是次要题材

Morisot painted *The Cradle* in 1872. It shows her sister Edma watching over Edma's sleeping daughter Blanche. The mother's bent arm echoes the baby's position, while the translucent curtain both connects and separates mother, child, painter, and viewer.

莫里索于1872年创作《摇篮》。画中，她的姐姐 Edma 注视着熟睡的女儿 Blanche。母亲弯曲的手臂呼应婴儿的姿势，半透明的帘幕则同时连接并分隔母亲、孩子、画家与观看者。

Shown at the 1874 exhibition, the painting was scarcely noticed and did not sell. It remained in the family until the Louvre acquired it in 1930. Its later fame can make the subject appear safely traditional: a mother tenderly watching a child. But the painting is also an experiment in looking. The veil interrupts unrestricted visual access to the baby, and Edma's concentrated gaze does not ask the spectator for approval.

这幅画参加了1874年展览，当时几乎没有受到关注，也未能售出。它一直保留在家族中，直到1930年被卢浮宫购藏。作品后来的名声容易让这个题材显得安全而传统：一位母亲温柔地注视孩子。但它同样是一场关于观看的实验。帘幕阻止观众毫无限制地接近婴儿，Edma 专注的目光也没有向观看者寻求认可。

Motherhood here is neither allegory nor sentimental decoration. It is an embodied relation structured by vigilance, fatigue, protection, distance, and attention. Morisot makes intimate experience carry the formal and psychological weight usually reserved for supposedly grander subjects.

这里的母职既不是寓言，也不是感伤性的装饰，而是一种由警觉、疲惫、保护、距离与注意力共同构成的身体关系。莫里索让亲密经验承担起通常只属于所谓重大题材的形式与心理重量。

## The Spaces Available to a Bourgeois Woman｜资产阶级女性能够进入的空间

Morisot's paintings repeatedly move among interiors, thresholds, gardens, balconies, public parks, boats, and seaside resorts. These were not the only places she found visually interesting. They were also among the places where a respectable woman of her class could work, observe, and return with models drawn from family, friends, servants, and paid caregivers.

莫里索的绘画不断往返于室内、门槛、花园、阳台、公共公园、船只与海滨度假地。这些不只是她在视觉上感兴趣的地方，也是她所属阶级的体面女性能够工作、观察，并带着家人、朋友、佣人或有偿照护者充当模特的空间。

Feminist art historian Griselda Pollock argues that nineteenth-century modernity was experienced through unequal access to space and to the act of looking. Bourgeois men could enter cafés, bars, backstage areas, and sites of sexual commerce without suffering the same damage to respectability that women risked. The celebrated “painting of modern life” was therefore already gendered.

女性主义艺术史家 Griselda Pollock 指出，十九世纪的现代性通过不平等的空间准入与观看权被体验。资产阶级男性可以进入咖啡馆、酒吧、后台与性交易场所，而不必承受女性可能遭遇的同等名誉损害。因此，那种备受赞扬的“现代生活绘画”从一开始就已经受到性别塑造。

This does not make Morisot's world less modern. It changes the map of modernity. Bedrooms, dressing rooms, gardens, and balconies become places where people negotiate privacy, leisure, labour, fashion, childcare, class position, and the possibility of being seen.

这并没有让莫里索笔下的世界变得不够现代，而是改变了现代性的地图。卧室、梳妆室、花园与阳台成为人们协商隐私、休闲、劳动、时尚、育儿、阶级位置与被观看可能性的场所。

## A Woman Looking, Not Merely Being Looked At｜观看的女人，而不只是被观看的女人

Morisot's place in art history has often been filtered through Édouard Manet's portraits of her. He painted her repeatedly, most famously in black dress and hat with a bouquet of violets. Those works are important, but they can turn a working artist back into a compelling face in a male painter's story.

莫里索在艺术史中的位置经常通过 Édouard Manet 为她创作的肖像被过滤。Manet 多次描绘她，其中最著名的是她身穿黑衣、戴黑帽并佩戴紫罗兰花束的形象。这些作品十分重要，却也可能把一位工作的艺术家重新变成男性画家故事中一张迷人的脸。

Their relationship involved exchange, admiration, disagreement, and unequal authority. In 1870, when Morisot asked for advice about *The Mother and Sister of the Artist*, Manet did not merely comment; he extensively repainted the figure of her mother. The surviving canvas makes the difference between their hands visible.

他们的关系包含交流、赞赏、分歧与不平等的权威。1870年，莫里索就《艺术家的母亲与姐姐》征求建议时，Manet 并不只是发表评论，而是大幅改画了她母亲的形象。现存画布至今仍让两人的笔触差异可见。

The episode should not be used to deny Morisot's agency or reduce Manet to a simple villain. It reveals how easily collegial advice could become intervention when a woman's confidence and professional authority were more fragile. Morisot later made one of the defining choices of her career by joining the independent exhibition despite Manet's objections.

这个事件不应被用来否定莫里索的能动性，也不应把 Manet 简化成一个反派。它揭示的是：当女性的职业信心与权威更加脆弱时，同侪建议多么容易转化成直接干预。后来，莫里索不顾 Manet 的反对参加独立展览，作出了界定自身艺术生涯的重要选择。

## The Politics of the “Feminine Touch”｜“女性笔触”的政治

Contemporary critics frequently described Morisot through flowers, charm, grace, delicacy, nervousness, and instinct. Some admired her; others used femininity to explain why her work should occupy a secondary category. In both cases, gender could replace sustained analysis of technique.

同时代评论家经常用花朵、魅力、优雅、纤细、神经质与直觉描述莫里索。有些评论带着赞赏，另一些则借“女性气质”解释她的作品为什么应当属于次要类别。在这两种情况下，性别都可能取代对技法的认真分析。

Her brushwork was quick and open, but quickness did not mean absence of thought. Preparatory drawings, repeated studies, changes of format, and abandoned canvases show sustained decision-making. The appearance of immediacy was produced through work.

她的笔触迅疾而开放，但迅疾并不意味着缺乏思考。准备素描、重复习作、画幅变化与被放弃的画布，都显示出持续的判断过程。看似即时的效果其实由劳动产生。

The question of finish was central to Impressionism as a whole. Morisot's pale grounds and exposed strokes pushed it especially far: figures sometimes seem to emerge and dissolve at once. To call this merely feminine sensitivity is to naturalise an artistic problem she pursued deliberately.

“完成度”是整个印象派的核心问题。莫里索的浅色底层与外露笔触把这个问题推进得格外远：人物有时像在出现的同时又开始消散。把这种效果仅仅称为女性的敏感，等于把她有意追求的艺术问题自然化。

## Women at Work, at Rest, and Between Roles｜劳动、休息与角色之间的女性

Morisot's women read, sew, dress, rest, care for children, sit in gardens, look through windows, and occupy boats. They are not all versions of one universal female experience. Family members, bourgeois friends, domestic workers, nannies, and models entered her work through different material relationships to leisure and labour.

莫里索笔下的女性阅读、缝纫、梳妆、休息、照料孩子、坐在花园里、望向窗外或置身船中。她们并不是某种普遍女性经验的不同版本。家人、资产阶级朋友、家务劳动者、保姆与职业模特，以不同的物质关系进入休闲与劳动，也进入她的画面。

This difference matters. A bourgeois woman seated at her toilette and a caregiver feeding another woman's child may share an interior, but they do not possess the same time, wages, privacy, or social mobility. Morisot's paintings can make their proximity visible without automatically resolving the inequality between them.

这种差异十分重要。坐在梳妆台前的资产阶级女性，与喂养另一个女人孩子的照护者可能共享同一室内空间，却并不拥有相同的时间、工资、隐私与社会流动性。莫里索的绘画能够让她们之间的接近变得可见，却不会自动解决其中的不平等。

Nor should every image of domestic life be declared feminist simply because a woman painted it. The stronger feminist question asks how Morisot organised looking: whether her figures return the gaze, turn away, withdraw into thought, protect another body, or remain absorbed in an activity that does not exist for the viewer.

我们也不应因为画家是女性，就把每一幅家庭生活图像都直接宣布为女性主义作品。更有力的女性主义问题应当追问莫里索如何组织观看：画中人物是回望、转身、沉入思绪、保护另一个身体，还是专注于一种并不为观众而存在的活动？

## Marriage Did Not End the Career｜婚姻没有结束她的职业生涯

Morisot married Eugène Manet, Édouard's brother, in 1874. Their daughter Julie was born in 1878. Unlike many nineteenth-century women whose artistic practice diminished after marriage, Morisot continued to exhibit, experiment, travel, and work professionally.

莫里索于1874年与 Édouard 的弟弟 Eugène Manet 结婚，女儿 Julie 于1878年出生。与许多婚后艺术实践逐渐减少的十九世纪女性不同，莫里索继续参展、实验、旅行并保持职业创作。

Eugène supported her work and frequently accompanied her while she painted. Julie became one of Morisot's most sustained subjects, but the relationship should not be reduced to a mother recording her beloved child. Across years of images, Julie appears as infant, reader, musician, adolescent, model, and developing person. Repetition becomes a study of time.

Eugène 支持她的工作，也经常在她作画时陪伴。Julie 成为莫里索最持久的题材之一，但这组关系不应被简化成母亲记录心爱的孩子。在多年图像中，Julie 以婴儿、读者、演奏者、少女、模特与逐渐成长的人出现。重复由此成为对时间的研究。

Family life provided subjects and cooperation, but it was not outside artistic labour. Models had to be available; childcare had to be arranged; studios, journeys, materials, exhibitions, sales, and correspondence required organisation. Morisot's career makes visible the infrastructure hidden behind the supposedly spontaneous Impressionist moment.

家庭生活提供了题材与合作，却并不位于艺术劳动之外。模特需要安排，儿童需要照护，工作室、旅行、材料、展览、销售与通信也需要组织。莫里索的职业生涯让所谓印象派即时瞬间背后的基础条件获得可见性。

## Recognition and the Problem of Art-Historical Memory｜承认，以及艺术史记忆的问题

Morisot was respected within the Impressionist circle and maintained a substantial professional career. It would be false to describe her as completely unknown in her lifetime. Yet recognition was repeatedly filtered through gendered language, family relationships, and comparisons with male colleagues.

莫里索在印象派圈内受到尊重，也拥有重要的职业生涯。把她描述成生前完全不为人知并不准确。但她获得的承认不断受到性别化语言、家庭关系与男性同侪比较的过滤。

Her posthumous exhibition history shows a similar imbalance. A memorial retrospective followed in 1896, but when the Musée d'Orsay organised its major 2019 exhibition, it was the museum's first monographic show devoted to her and the first solo presentation of her work by a French national museum since 1941.

她去世后的展览史也显示出类似的不平衡。1896年曾举办纪念性回顾展，但 Musée d'Orsay 于2019年举行大型展览时，那仍是该馆第一次为她举办个人专题展，也是在1941年以后法国国家级博物馆首次单独展出她的作品。

Recovery should mean more than adding one woman's name to a familiar list of Monet, Renoir, Degas, and Manet. Morisot changes the history of Impressionism by showing that its experiments with modern life, fleeting perception, mobility, leisure, and unfinished form were also experiments conducted from gendered spaces.

重新发现她，不应只是把一个女性姓名补进 Monet、Renoir、Degas 与 Manet 组成的熟悉名单。莫里索改变了印象派的历史：她说明这场运动对现代生活、瞬间感知、流动、休闲与未完成形式的实验，也是在受到性别塑造的空间中进行的实验。

## Quotation｜引文

> “I will gain my independence only through perseverance and by openly declaring my intention to emancipate myself.”
>
> “我只有依靠坚持，并公开表明我要获得解放的意志，才能取得自己的独立。”

— Berthe Morisot, 1871; translated from the French

Morisot wrote this at a decisive moment before the first Impressionist exhibition. Independence does not appear as an inner feeling that other people will eventually recognise. It requires duration, public intention, and an active claim to professional identity.

莫里索在第一届印象派展览之前的关键阶段写下这句话。独立在这里并不是一种终有一天会被别人发现的内在感受，而需要时间、公开表达，以及对职业身份的主动主张。

The sentence also resists the comforting idea that talent naturally defeats exclusion. Morisot had ability, training, money, and cultural connections, yet still understood emancipation as something that had to be repeatedly asserted.

这句话也拒绝一种令人安心却并不真实的想法：仿佛才能自然会战胜排斥。莫里索拥有能力、训练、金钱与文化关系，却仍然把解放理解成必须反复争取的事情。

Her persistence did not make gender irrelevant. It made possible a body of work through which we can see how gender entered artistic training, exhibition, criticism, subject matter, movement through the city, professional naming, and historical memory.

她的坚持并没有让性别变得无关紧要，而是创造出一批作品，使我们能够看见性别如何进入艺术教育、展览、批评、题材、城市移动、职业命名与历史记忆。

Biographical dates, Salon participation, the 1874 exhibition, Morisot's attendance at seven of eight Impressionist exhibitions, and her institutional position were checked against the [Musée d'Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en/program/whats-on/exhibitions/presentation/berthe-morisot-1841-1895), the [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/artists/1733-berthe-morisot), the [National Gallery, London](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/artists/berthe-morisot), and the [National Museum of Women in the Arts](https://nmwa.org/art/artists/berthe-morisot/).

生卒年份、沙龙参展、1874年独立展览、八届印象派展览中参加七届的记录及其制度位置，均通过[Musée d'Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en/program/whats-on/exhibitions/presentation/berthe-morisot-1841-1895)、[美国国家美术馆](https://www.nga.gov/artists/1733-berthe-morisot)、[伦敦国家美术馆](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/artists/berthe-morisot)与[美国国家女性艺术博物馆](https://nmwa.org/art/artists/berthe-morisot/)交叉核对。

The account of women's exclusion from the École des Beaux-Arts until 1897 was checked against the [Metropolitan Museum of Art's Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History](https://www.metmuseum.org/fr/essays/women-artists-in-nineteenth-century-france). Details concerning *The Cradle* come from the [Musée d'Orsay's collection record](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en/artworks/le-berceau-1132). Manet's intervention in *The Mother and Sister of the Artist* and Morisot's financial support for the Impressionist group were checked against the [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/46661-mother-and-sister-artist).

女性直到1897年才获准进入巴黎美术学院的情况，参照[大都会艺术博物馆 Heilbrunn 艺术史年表](https://www.metmuseum.org/fr/essays/women-artists-in-nineteenth-century-france)核实；《摇篮》的作品资料来自[Musée d'Orsay 馆藏页面](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en/artworks/le-berceau-1132)；Manet 对《艺术家的母亲与姐姐》的直接修改，以及莫里索为印象派团体提供经济支持的记录，则参照[美国国家美术馆](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/46661-mother-and-sister-artist)。

The quotation is translated from Morisot's 1871 French statement reproduced by the Musée d'Orsay: “Je n'obtiendrai [mon indépendance] qu'à force de persévérance et en manifestant très ouvertement l'intention de m'émanciper.” The English and Chinese versions above are calendar translations rather than quotations from a published bilingual edition.

引文译自 Musée d'Orsay 收录的莫里索1871年法语原句：“Je n'obtiendrai [mon indépendance] qu'à force de persévérance et en manifestant très ouvertement l'intention de m'émanciper.” 上方英文与中文均为本日历翻译，并非出自已经出版的双语版本。

The interpretation of gendered space draws on Pollock's [“Modernity and the Spaces of Femininity”](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/edit/10.4324/9780429492839-15/modernity-spaces-femininity-griselda-pollock). This calendar does not claim that domestic subjects are inherently feminine or automatically feminist. It asks how Morisot transformed the spaces available to her into a distinct account of modern life.

关于性别化空间的解释参考 Pollock 的[“Modernity and the Spaces of Femininity”](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/edit/10.4324/9780429492839-15/modernity-spaces-femininity-griselda-pollock)。本日历并不声称家庭题材天然属于女性，或只要由女性创作就自动成为女性主义艺术，而是追问莫里索如何把自己能够进入的空间转化成一种独特的现代生活叙述。
