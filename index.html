Women and Puppetry

Who Is Allowed to Animate the World?

谁被允许赋予世界生命？

Women and Puppetry asks us to look beyond the figure onstage. A puppet may appear to be only cloth, wood, paper, or string, but its body is never culturally empty. Audiences learn to read gender into its face, costume, movement, voice, and story—and often treat an unmarked body as male by default.

《女性与木偶：批评与历史研究》要求我们把目光移到舞台形象之外。一个木偶看似只是布、木头、纸或线，但它的身体从来不是文化上的空白。观众会从面孔、服装、动作、声音与故事中读出性别，也常常默认没有明显性别标记的身体是男性。
