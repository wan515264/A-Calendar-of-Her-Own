Who Has the Right to Tell Macabéa's Story?

谁有权讲述玛卡贝娅的人生？

Clarice Lispector tells the story of Macabéa, a poor migrant typist in Rio de Janeiro, through a male narrator who cannot describe her without exposing his own privilege. The novel asks whether literature can make an overlooked life visible without taking possession of it.

克拉丽丝·李斯佩克朵通过一位男性叙述者，讲述里约热内卢贫困移民打字员玛卡贝娅的人生；而他每一次描述她，也都暴露出自己的特权。小说追问：文学能否让被忽视的生命变得可见，却不把她据为己有？
